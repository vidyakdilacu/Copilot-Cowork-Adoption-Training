Reach for Chat whenyou need one answer, summary or draft from what's in front of you, and you'll use it straight away.
Reach for Cowork whenthe job has several steps, pulls from more than one place, and should end in a document, deck, tracker or set of messages you review.

Tip: you can also click a task and then click a column. With the keyboard, focus a task and press 1 for Chat, 2 for Cowork or 0 to send it back.
