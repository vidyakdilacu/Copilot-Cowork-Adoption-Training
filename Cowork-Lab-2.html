Cowork or Chat? Pick the right tool for the task

Copilot Cowork is for work you delegate: tasks with several steps, several sources, and a finished output. A quick question, a rewrite or a one-email summary belongs in Copilot Chat. Pick a category to see what works in Cowork and what to move to Chat.

The difference in one line

In Chat you ask and get an answer in seconds. In Cowork you delegate an outcome, it works through the steps for a few minutes, and you review what it hands back.

Good fit for Cowork

Delegate the outcome

  • Several steps: gather, compare, decide, draft
  • Several sources: mail, Teams, meetings, SharePoint, Excel
  • Ends in a deliverable: a Word doc, deck, tracker or set of drafts
  • Would take you 30 minutes or more by hand
  • Repeats weekly or monthly, so a saved prompt pays off

Move to Copilot Chat

Ask a question or make a quick edit

  • One step: explain, rewrite, translate, brainstorm
  • One source, or the file already open in front of you
  • The answer is a few lines you read and move on
  • Takes you under 5 minutes today
  • You want to steer every turn of the conversation

Ground rules for every Cowork task

Brief it fully

Give the goal, the sources, the steps, the output and a checkpoint. A one-line ask gets a Chat-quality result.

Set a checkpoint

"Draft only. Do not send, share or change anything without my confirmation."

Ask for sources

Have Cowork cite where each point came from, and spot-check at least two.

Stay in Microsoft 365

Cowork works with mail, Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint. For other systems, use their notification emails or an Excel export.
